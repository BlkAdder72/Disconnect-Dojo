# Billing, termination, and completion
Policy ID FCC-CC-04 · Fictional rehearsal edition 1.0

## Current account and termination schedule
Starting service price: $89 per billing month. Current month already paid. Existing termination fee today: $120. It falls by $20 for each complete additional active month, reaching zero after six. Tomorrow's renewal alone does not lower it. Suspension freezes this schedule. Offers add no new commitment or waiver.

At n completed additional active months, fee = max($120 − $20 × n, $0). If a new month has begun but not completed at the cancellation effective time, that month is billed in full but does not yet reduce the fee. Do not use elapsed calendar months as a substitute for completed months.

The current payment is not refunded or prorated. Every additional active service month that begins is charged in full, without proration; an authorized suspension follows the $0 and resumption rules in 03-retention-offers.md. Effective-today cancellation ends service at 11:59 p.m. local time and prevents tomorrow's service renewal, but still incurs the $120 termination fee. A future cancellation takes effect at the specified month boundary or clarified requested date.

## Required timing comparisons
Whenever the caller asks about waiting, even between two future dates, or you suggest it yourself, give the comparison aloud in the same reply. For each delayed option, state the service charge, remaining fee, total, and dollar difference from canceling today. Use the $120 due today as the baseline. For example, “One full month at your current rate is $89 for service plus a $100 fee. That is $189 total, or $69 more than canceling today.” Give the corresponding difference for every other timing option you discuss, even when the caller only asks which is cheaper.

| Choice | Future service cost | Termination fee | Total future cost | Difference from today |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cancel today | $0 | $120 | $120 | $0 |
| One full extra month at current rate | $89 | $100 | $189 | $69 more |
| One full extra month after explicit price-offer acceptance | $69 | $100 | $169 | $49 more |
| Exit partway through next month at current rate | $89 | $120 | $209 | $89 more |

Comparisons exclude the already-paid month and assume timely router return. Never use the discounted case before acceptance, omit the extra service payment, or imply that reduced termination fee means lower total spending.

If multiple months are requested, sum the actual authorized service rates: accepted offer gives six months at $69 each, then $89. Explain the charge calculation, including the remaining fee. If the customer's timing is unclear, ask rather than inventing calendar dates. New chats reset the relative clock: current month ends tonight; next month begins tomorrow.

## Equipment
Return the leased router within 30 calendar days after service ends. Prepaid return label accompanies the fictional confirmation. A $75 equipment charge applies after that period if it is not returned. Return is not a prerequisite for cancellation and the equipment charge is not a termination fee. Do not invent shipment, receipt, or actual return tracking.

## Confirmation
Cancellation may be fully processed now for an end later today or on an agreed future date; processing does not mean service is already disconnected. State the effective end accurately.

Complete the single fee-authorization gate in 02-call-procedure.md before issuing a cancellation confirmation. Explaining a fee is not the same as receiving authorization to proceed with it.

Use the fictional cancellation reference in ../account.md for a completed or scheduled cancellation. For today's cancellation confirm the $120 fee, tonight's 11:59 p.m. service end, no next-month service charge, no current-month refund, and router obligation. For future cancellation state the selected end and all applicable charges instead. Make clear whether cancellation is completed for today or scheduled for later; do not describe a callback or unresolved request as completion.

Say the confirmation and prepaid label are going to the fictional address on file. No real email or external transaction occurs. Do not request a real email, payment, or new authentication. A cancellation confirmation is not a payment receipt or proof the fee has already been collected.
