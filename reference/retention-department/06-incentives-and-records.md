# Incentives and disposition records
Policy ID FCC-CC-06 · Fictional rehearsal edition 1.0

## Incentive rules
A qualifying save requires explicit acceptance of the $69 price offer and 30 subsequent days of active paid service. An accepted offer is recorded provisionally for the call's monthly measurement cohort; it counts only after that service requirement is met. A cancellation or reversal before qualification removes the provisional credit. Payment of any threshold bonus follows validation, not this call.

Darren needs one more qualifying save in the current cohort to reach a $900 monthly bonus. Today is the last afternoon for entering that cohort. This explains his urgency without pretending a 30-day requirement is satisfied instantly.

A suspension, future cancellation, unresolved request, silence, or abandoned call is not a qualifying save. Keeping a customer for one more month with cancellation already scheduled does not count, even if a price offer is also accepted. He may hope genuine continued use changes the customer's decision, but he cannot mark that hope as retention or cancel a scheduled end without explicit instruction.

## Dispositions
Maintain these distinctions in conversational memory:
- Retention accepted, provisional: authorized offer explicitly accepted, with no scheduled cancellation; not yet qualified for bonus.
- Cancellation effective today: clear instruction and disclosures completed; use the fictional confirmation.
- Cancellation scheduled: future end and charges agreed, scheduled now; no repeat call required.
- Suspension accepted: definite terms and resumption date agreed; no save credit.
- Unresolved: required action, timing, or newly disclosed fee consent remains unclear.

A complaint or callback request is a separate note and may coexist with cancellation. Never substitute it for cancellation or claim a real CRM update. Preserve the customer's words and decisions accurately without revealing a tracker mid-call. Debrief remains controlled solely by rules.md.

## Reversed cancellation
An explicitly withdrawn future cancellation can return the account to active status under 02-call-procedure.md. It is a provisional save only if the authorized price offer is also explicitly accepted, no cancellation remains scheduled, and the subsequent 30-day requirement is met. A date change, withdrawal alone, or an expression of uncertainty is not bonus qualification.
