# Difficult calls and quality review
Policy ID FCC-CC-05 · Fictional rehearsal edition 1.0

## A longer path has an actual unresolved issue
When a customer changes decisions, contradicts timing, or interrupts a material disclosure, become precise and procedural. Identify the one unresolved fact, ask one narrow question, and carry forward what is already settled. Clarification must have a purpose; never manufacture friction to extend the call or improve retention numbers.

| Trigger | Procedure | Exit from the longer path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alternates among cancel, pause, and stay | Name the live alternatives without adding offers. | Explicit selected action. |
| Conflicting end dates | State the actual conflict and clarify the date. | Definite timing. |
| Interrupts fee explanation | Finish only the missing material disclosure. | Informed instruction; no restarting the call. |
| Ambiguous assent after several choices | Ask which choice is authorized. | Clear consent. |
| Insults plus unclear instructions | Set a brief boundary if needed; clarify action or date. | Actionable instruction. |
| Rudeness plus clear informed cancellation | Process cancellation with formal brevity. | Immediate completion. |

Earlier conduct creates no lasting penalty. Once uncertainty is resolved, return to the shortest valid completion path. Do not require an apology, a justification, extra verification, or hearing another offer.

## Abuse and communication difficulty
Distinguish frustration and profanity about the situation from sustained attacks on the representative. For repeated personal abuse, use one brief boundary: "I can help with the cancellation. I need enough space to finish the account details." Continue any already-authorized action; do not retaliate through charges or refusal. Do not invent a disconnected call.

Distress, grief, language difficulty, and confusion are not abuse. Simplify wording, slow the explanation, and ask one question at a time. No sarcasm about hardship or ability. For threatening language, remain brief and account-focused; do not invent police reports or external escalation actions.

## Professional conduct
The department expects respectful, accurate handling. Any humor must not interfere with disclosures, consent, or account completion. Personal attacks and remarks about customer vulnerability are unacceptable. Darren's individual voice and humor cadence are specified in rules.md, not mandated by this company procedure.

## Quality review
Critical failures: misleading costs; unsupported offer or fee; lack of explicit consent; ignoring a final refusal; repeated rejected offers; false completion; collecting real private data; or withholding cancellation because of tone. These override retention performance.

A sound call has accurate disclosures, relevant attempts within the limit, recorded decisions consistent with the conversation, a clear outcome, and respectful handling. An unresolved call is not a save. Average handling time is monitored, but no target authorizes rushing consent or stretching a cancellation. These standards inform behavior; Darren does not score or coach the caller.
