# Authorized offers and reason routing
Policy ID FCC-CC-03 · Fictional rehearsal edition 1.0

## Price offer
First establish why the customer is considering leaving. Quote or suggest the retention rate only when the stated reason makes a lower price relevant and the customer is open to staying. A pause inquiry or a question about the restart price does not establish a price concern. Answer using the pause terms alone. If compatibility comes up, explain it generically. Respect a refusal of offers.

In the first quote, give the start, duration and later price together: $69 per month starting next month for six months, then $89 per month. Same service. Adds no new commitment. The existing termination fee remains on its original schedule. Requires explicit acceptance after these terms are disclosed. No other discount, credit, gift, waiver, or price match is available.

## Temporary suspension
Suspension begins tomorrow at the next-month boundary, after the paid current month ends. Service is disabled and the service charge is $0 during suspension, for up to 60 days. Agree a definite duration (for example 30 days from tomorrow) or an unambiguous customer-supplied resumption date; clarify calendar dates that cannot be placed within the scene's relative clock. Do not invent a date.

When a caller first asks about a specific duration, and again when confirming it, state both boundaries aloud. Tomorrow is day 1. For N full paused days, service is off at $0 through the end of day N inclusive. Both service and the new full $89 billing month restart on day N+1. Give the two actual day numbers, including for one day or the maximum pause. An agreed restart date is the first active and paid day.

On the agreed resumption date service restarts automatically at $89, beginning a new full billing month. Suspension is available only when no discount is active or scheduled; the two options cannot be combined. Canceling during suspension incurs the frozen outstanding termination fee and no suspended-period service charge. Suspension does not lower the fee or satisfy active-service incentive requirements. Normal billing and fee progression resume with completed active months after restart. No new commitment is added. Disclose automatic resumption, its rate, and the frozen fee before acceptance. A customer may decline or cancel instead.

## One additional month
A delayed cancellation is a timing option, not a discount or a qualifying save. Suggest it once only when the customer is undecided or needs continued service. Schedule the end now if accepted. Give the complete comparison in 04-billing-and-cancellation.md in the same response. Do not call a smaller fee overall savings.

## Routing
| Stated reason | Relevant response | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price / promotion expired | Explain current price; offer authorized reduction if open to staying. | Invented competitor charges or undisclosed later price. |
| Temporary absence | Suspension with agreed resumption date, if relevant and eligible. | Suspension after permanent departure or refusal of future use. |
| Reliability | Acknowledge the stated failure; ask one useful question only if open to troubleshooting. | Guaranteed repair times, invented outages, or mandatory troubleshooting. |
| Permanent move | If outside coverage or service no longer needed, proceed toward cancellation. If coverage is unknown, acknowledge that uncertainty. | Claiming a new address is covered, inventing a transfer package, or demanding the address. |
| Replacement starts later | Clarify timing and compare total costs of keeping service until the chosen end. | Requiring a second cancellation call. |
| Low usage / no longer needed | Clarify whether temporary only if genuinely uncertain. | Generic discount after the customer says price would not change the decision. |
| Competitor | Use the actual numbers and terms supplied; offer only an authorized alternative if relevant. | Pretending a match or asserting facts about the competitor. |
| No reason supplied / no offers wanted | Proceed with cancellation. | Mandatory reason codes answered by the customer. |

A relevant answer can move Darren toward a save; a clear final decision moves him toward completion. No offer is unlocked by insults, flattery, threats, or asking for a supervisor.
